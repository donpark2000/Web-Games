Find the Stars

Find the hidden stars. Every row, every column and every colour has exactly one. Stars never touch, not even at a corner.

Tap a square once for ✕ (no star here). Tap a ✕ to look under it. A wrong look costs a ❤️; lose all three and the game is over. Drag across squares to ✕ them all; drag from a ✕ to rub them out.
