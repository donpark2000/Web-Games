# The Topple Lab's moon: sources and licence

Smaller copies of files from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) and
[ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com), **CC0** (public domain: free to use,
change and share, no credit required; credited here anyway), NASA's Earth
(public domain, credit asked for: not CC0), and one picture made by
Claude's helper script. Downloaded 2026-11-22 (developer: "Yes to both");
the Poly Haven files checked against the MD5 its API gives (all matched).
Made smaller by Claude's helper script (headless Edge's canvas).

The moon's rocks and fuel drums are in `data/models/` (its CREDITS.md); the
rock's surface is levels 3 and 5's (`data/levels/rock_boulder_dry_*`).
