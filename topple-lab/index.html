Topple Lab 🧪

A copy of Topple! for trying new looks and sounds. Its games, stars and settings are its own: Topple! on Let’s Play! isn’t touched.

Topple! (lab)

Being tried

  • The park (level 1) in photos: a real park all round, its ground the photo's own field, real leaves and bark on its tree and bushes, a branch slingshot.
  • The building site (level 2): the yard's own concrete for the ground, photographed barriers, drums and bags of cement round it.
  • Frosty Times (level 3): a real snowy field all round and underfoot, firs with needles and snow, a real stone, cracks in the ice.
  • Cactus Canyon (level 4): the desert photo's own sand underfoot, photographed boulders from the same desert, a ribbed cactus with spines, dry scrub.
  • Star Light (level 5): the snowy field at night, the real moon, sharp stars, pools of coloured light on the snow.
  • The moon (levels 6-10): real moon dust and rocks over craters, hills far off, panelled, worn metal, gold foil, the real Earth; drawn lighter on a slow device, as the park's levels are.
