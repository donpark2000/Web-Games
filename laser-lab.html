Laser Lab

Buy mirrors and splitters so the laser reaches every 💎, for as little as you can. A beam must never shine into a laser: it breaks.

Use as many pieces as you like: each one on the board costs its price, and taking it off gives the price back. Each puzzle has a budget; what's left of it when you solve it goes into your savings, over a run of 10 puzzles. Spend more and your savings pay. Tap a square to put the piece picked below the board there; tap it again to turn it, and once more to take it off. Tap a laser to switch it on: if its beam isn't right, it goes off and costs 1 coin. Moving a piece switches the lasers off. ❤️ Try again (3 in a run) starts a puzzle over with everything it cost given back; Show answer costs its whole budget.

The pieces

Mirror (1 coin each) turns the beam a quarter turn. Both sides reflect.
Splitter (2 coins each) lets the beam through and turns a copy of it, so one beam becomes two.
Walls and 💎 stop a beam. Beams of two colours pass through each other.
With two lasers, red and blue, a 💎 wants only its own colour; a purple one, half and half, wants both. Switch the lasers on one at a time: each beam must reach every 💎 of its colour, and no other.
