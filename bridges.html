Bridges

Join the islands with bridges. Each island needs as many bridges as its number.

Bridges go straight across or down, never cross, and at most two join the same two islands. In the end, every island is joined to the rest.

Tap between two islands for a bridge, again for two, once more to take them away. Or drag from an island.
