Colour Sudoku

Put 1 to 9 in every row, every column, every 3×3 box and every colour: each holds each digit once.

Tap a square, then a digit. A wrong digit costs a ❤️; lose all three and the game is over. The row of small dashed digits writes notes instead, to keep track of what could go where.
